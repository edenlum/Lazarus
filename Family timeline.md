# Family timeline

A working timeline based on the **scanned documents**, not an independent historical chronology. Dates and source pages are cited so later documents can confirm or challenge them.

## Childhood and marriage

- **1851, 18 July** — Birth of [[Aron Uriel Lazarus]]; date on photograph caption (Arthur memoir PDF p.3).
- **1853, 5 October** — Birth of [[Henriette Gitel Lazarus]], née Loewe (memoir p.3).
- **1886, 28 January** — Birth of [[Arthur Lazarus]] in [[Stargard]] (memoir pp.1–2).
- **1893, 13 January** — Death of Henriette, Arthur's mother, after a long illness (memoir p.3).
- **1893, 15 May** — Birth of [[Käte Lazarus]] (letters PDF p.1).
- **1913, 2 September** — Arthur and Käte marry. Their wedding photograph, menu and music program survive in the memoir (PDF pp.6–7; see [[1913]]).
- **1914, 22 September** — Birth of [[Walter Lazarus]] (memoir p.9).
- **1915, 14 January** — [[Hans Wolff]], Käte's brother, is killed during World War I (memoir p.8).
- **1915, April** — Arthur is called into the artillery; Käte remains in Berlin with baby Walter (memoir p.9).
- **1917, July 3** — Birth of [[Kurt Lazarus]] (memoir p.12, **still unverified OCR**).
- **1918, November** — Arthur returns to Berlin at the end of the war (memoir p.12, **still unverified OCR**).

## Years in Berlin

- **1921** — Arthur buys a plot in [[Dahlem]] during inflation (memoir p.14).
- **1922, 20 June** — Birth of [[Brigitte Lazarus]] in Treptow (memoir p.14).
- **1922, December** — Family moves into its house in Dahlem (memoir p.14).
- **1924–1933** — Period of family life, sports and holidays remembered fondly in [[Life in Dahlem]] (memoir pp.14–16).
- **1933, April** — Nazi-era turning point in Arthur's account; see [[1933]] and [[Departure from Germany]] (memoir pp.16–17).
- **1933, September** — Arthur and Käte visit Palestine aboard the **SS Roma** to assess emigrating (memoir p.17).
- **1936, November** — Family's **departure from Germany**, according to Arthur; not 1933 (memoir p.17; see [[1936]]).
- **1938** — Brigitte travels to England for further education (memoir p.17).
- **1939** — Brigitte returns as World War II begins, and later works in Jerusalem (memoir p.17; precise chronology within the year is not established).

## Palestine and Israel

- **1941–1949** — Arthur says he and Käte lived in [[Jerusalem]], having moved from [[Tel Aviv]] at Brigitte's request (memoir p.17).
- **1948, 1 May** — Käte describes the lack of outgoing mail and separation from family (letters PDF p.2).
- **1948, 5 May** — A letter dated 16 April finally reaches Käte in Jerusalem after 18 days; she describes the emotional impact of reading the family's words (letters p.7, opening paragraph visually checked).
- **1948, 15 June** — Käte reports enormous relief on receiving a family telegram and fear that the fighting might resume (letters p.31).
- **1948, 23 June** — Last dated letter in this compilation; she asks loved ones to preserve her diary and letters (letters pp.34–35).
- **1950 / 1951** — A house photograph in [[Shaar Hefer]] is captioned **1950** in Arthur's memoir (p.18) and **1951** in Käte's letters compilation (p.35). Same-looking image, *conflicting date captions*; see [[Photographs and caption conflicts]].
- **1952** — Family-reunion photographs at Shaar Hefer (memoir p.19).
- **1953, 7 July** — Arthur signs his memoir in Shaar Hefer (memoir p.20).
- **1957, 3 January** — Death of Arthur; death notice reproduced in memoir PDF p.20.
- **1971, 14 October** — Death of Käte (letters cover PDF p.1).
- **2006** — English translation of Arthur's memoir credited to Brigitte Paterson; photographs added by Ilana Maik (memoir PDF p.20). The 1948 letters were also later compiled with photographs and annotations.
