# Jerusalem

[[Arthur Lazarus]] says he and [[Käte Lazarus]] moved here from [[Tel Aviv]] at [[Brigitte Lazarus]]'s urging and lived in the city from **1941 to 1949** (memoir PDF p.17).

During **1948**, Käte recorded the personal effect of the siege in letters to relatives outside the city. In the letters visually checked so far, she describes a stopped postal service (1 May, PDF p.2), tremendous relief after a telegram from family (15 June, p.31), and the gradual return of supplies and a cinema outing (23 June, p.35).

Arthur's memoir also recalls water and food shortages, and his sons' encouragement to move to [[Shaar Hefer]] (p.18).
