# Tel Aviv

The first home in Palestine named by [[Arthur Lazarus]] after the family's **November 1936** departure from Germany. Arthur writes “We settled in Tel-Aviv,” then says [[Brigitte Lazarus]]'s work in Jerusalem and her request led the parents to move there. He dates their stay in [[Jerusalem]] to **1941–1949**.

**Source:** Arthur memoir PDF p.17, visually checked. The statement does not establish exactly where every child lived in each year.
