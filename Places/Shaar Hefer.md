# Shaar Hefer

Also written **Sha'ar Hefer**, **Shaar Hefer**, **שער חפר**, **Schaar Chefer**, and **Scha'ar Hefer** in the documents.

The rural home associated with [[Arthur Lazarus]] and [[Käte Lazarus]] after the siege of Jerusalem. In Arthur's memoir he says the sons—especially [[Walter Lazarus]]—encouraged their parents to move here. Arthur calls their house a bungalow, though he notes health complications and says Käte never fully adjusted to rural life (memoir PDF p.18).

The memoir reproduces a photograph of the house captioned **1950** (PDF p.18). The letters compilation reproduces what looks like **the same photograph** captioned **1951** (letters PDF p.35). Treat this as an unresolved caption-date conflict; see [[Photographs and caption conflicts]].

The memoir was signed here on **7 July 1953** (memoir p.20). Its reproduction of Arthur's 1957 death notice names "Schaar Chefer-Nira" (p.20).

The letters compilation includes further family photographs here dated 1945 and 1951 (letters PDF pp.2, 35).
