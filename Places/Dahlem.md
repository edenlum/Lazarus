# Dahlem

A suburb of Berlin where [[Arthur Lazarus]] and [[Käte Lazarus]] lived with their children, including [[Walter Lazarus]], [[Kurt Lazarus]] and [[Brigitte Lazarus]].

Arthur bought land during inflation in **1921**, and the family moved into its house in **December 1922**. The home had an orchard and a children's pool, and the sons attended the Arndt Gymnasium nearby. The memoir shows photographs of the house (1925), the children and relatives (1923–1934).

After April 1933 the family's plans changed. Arthur dates their departure from Germany to **November 1936** (memoir pp.14–17).
