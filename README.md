## Layers

- `Scans/` — **both original PDFs are uploaded and intact** (20-page memoir, 36-page letters). Source of truth.
- `Transcriptions/` — 56 page-indexed text blocks; **25 pages' main text visually reviewed** (the complete 20-page English memoir and 5 pages of Käte's Hebrew collection), plus partial checks on two additional Hebrew pages. Unchecked OCR sections and facsimiles remain.
- `People/`, `Years/`, `Places/`, `Stories/` — flexible interlinked Markdown notes, not a rigid database.
- `docs/` — the **curated mobile website** (Layer 4), separate from the private vault; activate GitHub Pages using [PUBLISHING.md](PUBLISHING.md).

## Important corrections discovered during verification

1. **Emigration:** the family began planning in **1933**, but Arthur says they departed Germany in **November 1936** (memoir PDF p.17).
2. **Letter recipients:** Käte's letters **are not shown to be addressed to Arthur**. He appears to have been with her in Jerusalem; exact addressees remain uncertain (letters PDF pp.1–2, 35).
3. **House photograph:** Shaar Hefer photo is dated **1950** in one compilation and **1951** in the other (memoir p.18, letters p.35).
4. **Source history:** the memoir is an English translation and illustrated edition produced in **2006** of a text signed in **1953** (memoir p.20). The letters are a later Hebrew translation of original German letters (letters p.36).
