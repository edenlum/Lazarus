# 1936

**November 1936:** [[Arthur Lazarus]] dates the family's departure from Germany to this month, following preparations begun in 1933 (memoir PDF p.17).

The memoir also describes a **September 1933** reconnaissance visit to Palestine by Arthur and [[Käte Lazarus]]. They subsequently settled in [[Tel Aviv]] and later in [[Jerusalem]].

**Note:** This is Arthur's retrospective date for the family's departure; it does not prove that all children emigrated together or on the same day.
