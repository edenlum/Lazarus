# 1933

**Important:** 1933 was a turning point and the start of emigration planning, **not** the year the Lazarus family finally left Germany.

- Arthur reports that events in April convinced him there was no future for his children in Germany (memoir PDF pp.16–17).
- [[Walter Lazarus]] had finished the Abitur and planned to start a railway apprenticeship; circumstances changed (p.16).
- The children began different education and training paths, described in [[Departure from Germany]] (p.17).
- In **September 1933**, [[Arthur Lazarus]] and [[Käte Lazarus]] traveled aboard the **SS Roma** to inspect Palestine as a possible new home (p.17).
- Arthur dates the actual **departure from Germany to November 1936** (p.17).
