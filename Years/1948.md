# 1948

[[Käte Lazarus]] wrote letters to [[Arthur Lazarus]] from Jerusalem between 1 May and 23 June 1948. See [[Letters from Jerusalem 1948]] for dated page anchors.

The compilation documents convoys, the siege, water and other shortages, and family life against wartime events. Individual incidents and dates require verification in the original Hebrew.

Source: `Scans/Kaete-Lazarus-Letters-1948.pdf` pp.1–36.
