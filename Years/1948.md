# 1948

[[Käte Lazarus]]'s letters to family members **outside Jerusalem** from **1 May to 23 June 1948** give an unusually personal view of family separation during the siege of [[Jerusalem]]. The cover does not name the recipients; Arthur appears to be with Käte in the city.

- **1 May:** The postal link has stopped; Käte describes the beauty of spring alongside the agony of not knowing how her children and grandchildren are (letters PDF p.2).
- **15 June:** A telegram from family members brings enormous relief. She writes that once her first fears are answered, new longings arise—to hear more and to see everyone again (p.31).
- **23 June:** She reports modest recovery in supplies and signs off with the hope that such bitter times never again separate their correspondence (p.35).

Other letter dates and historical events are indexed in [[Letters from Jerusalem 1948]], but not all pages have been visually verified.

**Source note:** Photos and footnotes in the compiled document may have been added years later. Do not treat a photograph dated 1951 as an image taken during the 1948 siege.
