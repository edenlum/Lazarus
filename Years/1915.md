# 1915

- **14 January:** Death of [[Hans Wolff]], brother of [[Käte Lazarus]], during World War I. His photograph and dates are printed in Arthur's memoir, PDF p.8.
- **April:** [[Arthur Lazarus]] is called into the German artillery and spends much of the war away from Käte and their infant son [[Walter Lazarus]], born the previous September (memoir p.9).
- The memoir includes a photograph of Käte with infant Walter captioned **1915** (p.9).
