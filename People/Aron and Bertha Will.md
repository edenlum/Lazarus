# Aron and Bertha Will

[[Arthur Lazarus]] writes that while his mother [[Henriette Gitel Lazarus]] was ill, he spent years of his early childhood with her half-brother **Uncle Aron** and his wife **Aunt Bertha Will**, who had no children and cared for him (Arthur memoir PDF p.3).

He reflects that they spoiled him, in contrast to his later feelings about his childhood home. This personal recollection is one of the more revealing passages in the memoir.

**Uncertainty:** The transcript describes Aron as Henriette's half-brother, but does not give an independently verified birth/death date or documentary family-tree evidence. This is a combined note for two people until more material is found.
