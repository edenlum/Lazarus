# Hans Wolff

**5 January 1895 – 14 January 1915**, dates as printed under his military portrait in [[Arthur Lazarus]]'s memoir (PDF p.8).

Arthur identifies Hans as the brother of his wife [[Käte Lazarus]]. The family received news of Hans's death in **January 1915**, during World War I; Arthur says he was the first man in his infantry company to be struck by a fatal bullet.

This is **Arthur's retrospective description**, not a verified military service record. The portrait and dates are reproduced in the memoir, PDF p.8.
