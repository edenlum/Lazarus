# Brigitte Lazarus

**Born 20 June 1922** in Treptow, Berlin (Arthur memoir PDF p.14). Daughter of [[Arthur Lazarus]] and [[Käte Lazarus]], sister of [[Walter Lazarus]] and [[Kurt Lazarus]].

Arthur recalls Brigitte's childhood in the family's garden house in [[Dahlem]] (memoir p.14). A photo of Käte holding baby Brigitte appears on p.14 (captioned 1923), and a photograph of the children and Käte appears on p.15 (captioned 1924).

In the early Nazi period, Brigitte moved to a Zionist school in Berlin. She went to England for further education in **1938** and returned in **1939**. Arthur writes that she matriculated through the University of London external system and later worked at Barclays Bank in Jerusalem; at her urging, the parents moved from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem (memoir p.17).

**Name ambiguity to investigate:** Arthur's 1957 German death notice lists **BRIGITTE CLAYTON**, while the 2006 English translation credit reads **Brigitte Paterson** (memoir p.20). These may reflect later surnames or different individuals; *do not treat as confirmed aliases without family evidence*.
