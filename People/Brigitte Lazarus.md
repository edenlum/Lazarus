# Brigitte Lazarus

**Born 20 June 1922** in Treptow, Berlin (Arthur memoir PDF p.14). Daughter of [[Arthur Lazarus]] and [[Käte Lazarus]], sister of [[Walter Lazarus]] and [[Kurt Lazarus]].

Arthur recalls Brigitte's childhood in the family's garden house in [[Dahlem]] (memoir p.14). Käte with baby Brigitte (captioned 1923) appears on p.14; the three children with Käte (1924) appear on p.15.

After 1933 Brigitte moved to a Zionist school in Berlin. She went to England for further education in 1938, returned in 1939, studied through the University of London external program and later worked at Barclays Bank in Jerusalem. Arthur says she urged her parents to move there from Tel Aviv (memoir p.17).

## Names and editorial role — newly reconciled

- A photograph in the 1948 letters compilation is captioned **"Brigitte and David Clayton – 1945"** (Hebrew compilation PDF p.7).
- Arthur's 1957 death notice lists **BRIGITTE CLAYTON** (memoir p.20).
- The English memoir was translated in 2006 by **Brigitte Paterson** (memoir p.20).
- **The editor's note on Käte letters PDF p.36 explicitly names “בריגיטה-לצרוס-קלייטון-פטרסון” (Brigitte Lazarus–Clayton–Paterson)**, linking the surnames as referring to the same person in the family's 2006 compilation.

The letters colophon credits Brigitte for transcribing/copying the letters and many explanatory notes in the margins (p.36). The **German-to-Hebrew translation** is separately credited to another contributor, not necessarily Brigitte.

This establishes a source-based name connection, **not** the dates or circumstances of any marriage/name change.
