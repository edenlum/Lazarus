# Johanna Wolff

An image in Arthur's memoir (PDF p.16) is captioned: **"Walter & Brigitte with their grandmother, Johanna Wolff 'Oti' — Dahlem -1933"**.

She is therefore **identified by the compilation's caption** as a grandmother of [[Walter Lazarus]] and [[Brigitte Lazarus]]. Whether she was Käte's mother, grandmother by marriage or another relative should be checked before building a more specific family-tree edge.

Käte's 1948 letter (PDF p.2) mentions visiting someone called **'אוטי'** and an editorial parenthesis describes that person as the writer's mother. This suggests a possible connection to *Oti*, but the source names and familial link need reconciliation.
