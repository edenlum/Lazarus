# Dora Lazarus

**DORA LAZARUS und Familien** appears in the German death notice reproduced in [[Arthur Lazarus]]'s memoir (PDF p.20), dated January 1957.

Her precise relationship to Arthur is **not established by that notice**, and should remain unresolved until further family documents identify her.

**Source:** memoir PDF p.20; manually checked transcription of the death notice (German).
