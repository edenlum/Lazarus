# Käte Lazarus

**15 May 1893 – 14 October 1971** (Hebrew letters cover PDF p.1). Also Kaete, Kate, קטה.

Wife of [[Arthur Lazarus]], married 2 September 1913 (Arthur memoir p.6). Mother of [[Walter Lazarus]], [[Kurt Lazarus]] and [[Brigitte Lazarus]].

[[Letters from Jerusalem 1948]] is a compilation of her correspondence to Arthur between 1 May and 23 June 1948, during the siege of Jerusalem. The letters describe wartime conditions, family relationships and daily life, interspersed with historical and family images. See [[1948]].

Sources: `Scans/Kaete-Lazarus-Letters-1948.pdf` pp.1–36; `Scans/Arthur-Lazarus-Memoir.pdf` pp.6, 14–19. Exact quotations require verified transcription.
