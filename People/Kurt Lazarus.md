# Kurt Lazarus

Son of [[Arthur Lazarus]] and [[Käte Lazarus]], and brother of [[Walter Lazarus]] and [[Brigitte Lazarus]].

Arthur says Kurt was born **3 July 1917** (memoir PDF p.12; that page's OCR still needs visual checking). A family portrait of Walter, Käte and Kurt dated 1918 is on p.12. Kurt appears in a 1933 Geneva photograph (p.15).

As the family prepared to leave Germany, he joined an agricultural training program (*Hachsharah*) on a farm in **Holland** (Arthur memoir p.17). Arthur later says both sons had joined kibbutzim after the family settled in Palestine (p.17).

Kurt is also mentioned in the 15 June 1948 letter as one of the family correspondents reporting from Haifa (letters PDF p.31); the exact relationship of the other names in that telegram must still be resolved.
