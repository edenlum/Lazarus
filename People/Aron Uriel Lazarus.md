# Aron Uriel Lazarus

**18 July 1851 – 6 February 1935**, as printed below his portrait in Arthur's memoir, PDF p.3.

Father of [[Arthur Lazarus]], married to [[Henriette Gitel Lazarus]]. Arthur reports that he was born in Vilna, left Russian territory, spent time in England and moved to Stargard in 1879. He ran a small business selling clothing and other items (memoir p.3).

The OCR draft had previously misread his death year as **1936**. The photograph caption and memoir say **1935**, which has been visually verified (PDF p.3).

The memoir reports his age as 83 when he died. The birth/death dates are consistent with that age.
