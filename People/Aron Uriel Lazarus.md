# Aron Uriel Lazarus

**18 July 1851 – 6 February 1935**, according to printed caption (Arthur memoir PDF p.3).

Father of [[Arthur Lazarus]]. Arthur describes his business background and time in England before arriving in Stargard, where he married [[Henriette Gitel Lazarus]].

Source: `Scans/Arthur-Lazarus-Memoir.pdf` p.3.
