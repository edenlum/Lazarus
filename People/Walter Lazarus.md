# Walter Lazarus

**Born 22 September 1914**, according to [[Arthur Lazarus]]'s memoir (PDF p.9). Son of Arthur and [[Käte Lazarus]]; brother of [[Kurt Lazarus]] and [[Brigitte Lazarus]].

As an infant he remained in Berlin with Käte after Arthur was called into military service in April 1915 (memoir p.9). Walter attended the Arndt Gymnasium in [[Dahlem]], passed the Abitur in 1933 and had been due to begin an apprenticeship with the Reichsbahn before the Nazi-era crisis (memoir pp.15–16).

During the family's preparations to emigrate, he trained in mechanical engineering first in Chemnitz and later in Winterthur, near Zurich (memoir p.17). Arthur writes that Walter, especially, urged his parents to move to [[Shaar Hefer]] from Jerusalem after the difficult siege months (memoir p.18).

A photo of Walter as an infant appears in the memoir, p.9. A 1934 photograph with Arthur appears on p.16. The 1951 photo caption in the letters compilation, PDF p.35, names Walter among a family group.

Käte's later letter (PDF p.34; OCR not yet verified) mentions Helen as Walter's wife. Verify that page before using the relationship as established.
