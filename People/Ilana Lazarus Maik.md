# Ilana Lazarus Maik

The closing credits of the **2006 compilation of [[Käte Lazarus]]'s letters** identify the editor as **"אילנה-לצרוס-מאיק נכדתה של קטה לצרוס"** — Ilana Lazarus Maik, a granddaughter of Käte (letters PDF p.36).

She thanks her aunt [[Brigitte Lazarus]] (named as Brigitte Lazarus–Clayton–Paterson), a translator of the letters from German into Hebrew, and contributors who supplied photographs and materials. The closing note is dated **August 2006**.

Photographs captioned Ilana or Ilana and Naomi appear in the letters volume, PDF pp.2 and 31, dated 1945 and 1947. The exact connection between every similarly named person and the editor should be checked against family testimony rather than inferred from first names alone.

The memoir separately credits **Ilana Maik** with adding photographs to Arthur's English translation in April 2006 (memoir PDF p.20). The combination of family name and cross-document credits is strong evidence of related editorial work.
