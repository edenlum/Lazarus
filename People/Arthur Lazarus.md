# Arthur Lazarus

**28 January 1886 – 3 January 1957** (memoir PDF p.1).

Arthur describes childhood in [[Stargard]], Pomerania. Parents: [[Aron Uriel Lazarus]] and [[Henriette Gitel Lazarus]] (pp.2–3). His mother died in 1893 (p.3). He apprenticed and worked in business including in Berlin (pp.4–5).

He married [[Käte Lazarus]] on 2 September 1913 (pp.6–7). Their children include [[Walter Lazarus]], [[Kurt Lazarus]] and [[Brigitte Lazarus]] (pp.9, 12, 14–17). He served in the German military during World War I and describes wartime decorations (pp.9–11).

His memoir describes the family's [[Departure from Germany]] following the Nazi rise in [[1933]] (p.17), subsequent life in Palestine, and eventual settlement at [[Shaar Hefer]] (pp.17–19). He signs the memoir on 7 July 1953 (p.20).
