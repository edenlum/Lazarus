# Georg Lazarus

The **German-language death notice** reproduced in Arthur's memoir (PDF p.20) lists **GEORG LAZARUS und Familien** among the names below [[Arthur Lazarus]]'s death announcement (January 1957).

The notice does **not** explicitly label Georg's relationship to Arthur. Do not assume he was a child or sibling without another source. Arthur's memoir identifies three children (Walter, Kurt and Brigitte), making it especially important not to add unsupported parent-child edges.

**Source:** memoir PDF p.20; manually checked transcription of the death notice (German).
