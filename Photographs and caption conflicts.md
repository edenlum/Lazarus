## Unresolved discrepancy: same-looking Shaar Hefer house

The house photograph in Arthur's memoir PDF **p.18** and in Käte's compilation PDF **p.35** appears to be the **same photograph**, but captions disagree:

- Arthur memoir: **“The house in Shaar Hefer - 1950.”**
- Käte compilation: **“הבית בשער חפר - 1951.”**

Possible explanations include later captioning errors or different dating conventions. The scans alone **do not resolve it**. Until more evidence appears, refer to the photo as **circa 1950–1951**.

## Editorial caution

The memoir was signed in 1953 but its photographs were added in 2006 (credit printed on PDF p.20). The letters are a retrospective **illustrated compilation** of 1948 texts with photographs from several years. Distinguish the photographed event, the photo caption, and the date of compilation.
