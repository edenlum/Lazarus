# Guidelines for AI agents editing the Lazarus archive

This is a flexible Markdown/Obsidian vault, **not** a strict database. **CAUTION: the current repository is PUBLIC**; do not assume scans or notes are private.

1. **Immutable sources:** Never rewrite, enhance, rename or delete files under `Scans/` without explicit instruction.
2. **Source hierarchy:** Scan image → visually checked transcription → raw OCR → source-linked synthesis. A synthesis is never a second independent source.
3. **Page citations:** Give original scan filename and **PDF page number** for substantive claims. Keep printed-page numbers distinct.
4. **No invented facts:** Preserve uncertain names, dates, places, kinship and translation ambiguities. Never silently finish unclear text with plausible content.
5. **OCR status:** Both documents have now had an initial 56/56-page visual main-text pass, **but not a second independent proofreading**; particularly difficult Hebrew phrases and foreign names still need checking. Read [[Sources and review status]] before quoting; label new proofread corrections precisely.
6. **Document provenance:** Arthur's memoir (1953) is preserved in a 2006 English translation with added photos; Käte's 1948 letters were originally German and are preserved in a 2006 Hebrew translation with later illustrations. Editorial notes and photographs are not necessarily contemporary evidence.
7. **Known traps:** Don't say the family emigrated in 1933 (Arthur says **November 1936**). Don't say Käte wrote *to Arthur* (the recipient is not established, and he appears to have been with her). Don't silently choose **1950** or **1951** for the house photograph.
8. **Historical voice:** Distinguish words and opinions of Arthur or Käte from external facts. Do not fabricate quotations, private thoughts, scenes or motives.
9. **Obsidian links:** Use `[[wikilinks]]` freely; add people, years, places and stories only when useful. Avoid rigid schemas and redundant stub creation.
10. **Aliases:** Käte/Kaete/Kate/קטה; Walter/ולטר; Na(o)mi/Noemi; Shaar Hefer/שער חפר. Do not automatically merge similarly named people without evidence.
11. **Living family/privacy:** This repository is currently **public** due to GitHub Pages; do not add new private family records, sensitive details or identifiable information about living relatives without permission. Recommend a private source-vault repository and a separate public site.
12. **Change notes:** For each corrected scan page, update `Sources and review status.md` with coverage and record material factual corrections in linked notes.
13. **Public website:** The `docs/` directory is a deliberately curated public-output surface. NEVER expose private `Scans/`, raw transcriptions, sensitive information or newly discovered details about living relatives there automatically. See `PUBLISHING.md`.
