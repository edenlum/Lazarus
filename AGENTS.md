# Instructions for AI agents

1. `Scans/` is immutable historical evidence. Never overwrite.
2. `Transcriptions/` faithfully preserves original wording, languages and uncertainty. Never silently paraphrase or complete missing text.
3. Cite source filename and **PDF page** for factual statements.
4. A generated summary is not independent evidence.
5. Mark identity, date and relationship uncertainties rather than guessing.
6. Preserve spelling variants such as Käte, Kaete, Kate and קטה.
7. Full transcriptions are pending review.
8. Do not present invented dialogue, scenes or AI imagery as archival evidence.
9. Link pages using Obsidian `[[wikilinks]]` without requiring a rigid schema.
10. Keep the repository private; photographs and stories may involve living family members.
