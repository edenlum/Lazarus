# Life in Dahlem

[[Arthur Lazarus]] acquired a plot of land in **1921** during inflation, and the family moved into their house in [[Dahlem]], Berlin, in **December 1922**. [[Brigitte Lazarus]], born in June 1922, grew up there (Arthur memoir PDF p.14).

Arthur recalls a garden that mattered to the family: fruit trees producing apples, pears, plums, peaches and more, plus a small swimming pool for the children. [[Walter Lazarus]] and [[Kurt Lazarus]] attended the Arndt Gymnasium, and holidays in the Alps were part of family life (memoir p.15).

The album-like memoir preserves a photograph of the Dahlem house in summer **1925** (p.14), a portrait of Käte with infant Brigitte in **1923** (p.14), a portrait of Käte with all three children in **1924** (p.15), and a scene with grandmother [[Johanna Wolff]] in **1933** (p.16).

Arthur's heading for the period is telling: **"EVER FORWARD AND UPWARD TO THE DOWNFALL"**. His retrospective memory of prosperity ends in the knowledge of what followed in 1933. Read this with [[Departure from Germany]], keeping the contrast rooted in *Arthur's own recollections*, not as proof that everyone in the household felt the same way.

**Source:** Arthur memoir, scanned PDF pp.14–16 (visually checked).
