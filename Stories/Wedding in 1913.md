# Wedding in 1913

[[Arthur Lazarus]] married [[Käte Lazarus]] on **Tuesday, 2 September 1913**. He was 27 and she was 20. The memoir reproduces their wedding portrait, menu and music program (PDF pp.6–7).

The details make this event unusually tangible. The printed menu lists caviar on ice, bone-marrow consommé, trout, duckling, asparagus, cheeses and desserts, alongside wines and champagne. The ten-piece music program includes Mendelssohn, Bizet, Weber, Wagner, Grieg and Brahms. These are **actual items printed in the later English rendering of the wedding documents**, not reconstructed decorations.

In his memoir, Arthur describes being newly married and full of hope—and then recalls how the 1914 war interrupted that confidence within a year (memoir PDF p.6).

**Sources:** Arthur memoir pp.6–7, visually checked. The German original menu appears as a photograph on p.6; its separately typeset English version is on p.7.
